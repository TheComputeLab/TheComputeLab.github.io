> **Experiment. Understand. Build. Deploy.**

**The Compute Lab** is an ongoing personal technical project focused on exploring, building, documenting, and understanding modern computing and artificial intelligence systems.

The goal is not simply to collect tutorials or definitions.

It is to understand **how things actually work**.

From data preparation and machine learning algorithms to computer vision, local AI infrastructure, GPU computing, model deployment, and real-world AI systems — The Compute Lab is being built as a continuously evolving technical knowledge base and experimentation environment.

---

## Why The Compute Lab?

Artificial intelligence is often presented as:

```text
Dataset
   ↓
Model
   ↓
Accuracy
```

But real systems are much larger than the model itself.

A practical AI system involves:

```text
DATA
 ↓
PREPROCESSING
 ↓
FEATURES
 ↓
MODEL
 ↓
EVALUATION
 ↓
INFERENCE
 ↓
DEPLOYMENT
 ↓
MONITORING
```

The Compute Lab exists to explore that complete journey.

# Learn Through Visuals

One of the principles of The Compute Lab is:

> **If a concept produces an output, show the output.**

Instead of only explaining:

```text
"Correlation measures the relationship
between two variables."
```

the lab should also show:

```text
Method
   ↓
Visualization
   ↓
How to read it
   ↓
What it means
   ↓
What it means for the model
```

This approach is being applied throughout the Machine Learning and Computer Vision labs.

Examples include:

- Histograms
- Scatter plots
- Regression plots
- Residual plots
- Confusion matrices
- ROC curves
- Precision-Recall curves
- Feature importance
- Correlation heatmaps
- Segmentation masks
- Detection bounding boxes
- Model architecture diagrams
- Deployment architecture diagrams

# The Philosophy

The core idea behind the project is simple:

> **Don't just use the technology. Understand what happens underneath.**

A model is only one component of a larger system.

Understanding the surrounding data, infrastructure, runtime, deployment and operational behavior is equally important.
